JCM ENTERPRISES WEBSITE
========================
Files:
- index.html: main website
- styles.css: responsive design
- script.js: WhatsApp ordering and mobile menu
- logo.svg: starter JCM Enterprises logo

To test:
1. Open index.html in Chrome/Edge.
2. Click the WhatsApp buttons to test ordering.

To publish:
- Buy a domain such as jcmenterprises.co.ke.
- Upload these files to your web hosting public_html folder.
- Ensure index.html is in the main public folder.

Before publishing, consider adding:
- Real photos of your rice and shop.
- Your exact Google Maps pin.
- Current prices and package sizes.
- A business registration/contact section if desired.
